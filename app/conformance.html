Point it at your own origin

Your origin is embedded here and asked what it can commit to. Nothing is written down, nothing is sent anywhere, and no step of any protocol is executed — the suite only reads declarations and asks a participant to describe itself. It has to be able to see them: a participant that names one origin in exposedTo, which is what every participant in this repository does, is invisible to every other origin including this one. Add this origin to yours while you test.

What the levels mean

L1 · declares
A concord.protocol tool, exposed to embedders, honest about which rung it is on.
L2 · recoverable
Plus concord.status, so an interrupted commitment can be resolved instead of guessed at, and steps that are safe to retry.
L3 · attesting
Plus statements signed with a key published on its own origin, so a receipt can be checked by someone who trusts neither party.
